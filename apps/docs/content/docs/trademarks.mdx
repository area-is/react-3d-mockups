The device mockups depict real products - Samsung Galaxy phones, foldables,
tablets and watches, and Apple iPhones, iPads, MacBooks, the Apple Watch and
the Studio Display. Those product names, logos, trademarks and industrial
designs belong to their respective owners. They are named in this library and
on this site only to identify what each model depicts.

## What the models are

Every device is an original, procedural approximation - geometry generated in
code at runtime, built from published specifications and product photography.
No manufacturer's CAD data, 3D files or artwork are used. The models are close
enough for design work, but they are not dimensionally exact and they are not
official product models.

react-3d-mockups is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Samsung,
Apple, or any other manufacturer whose products it depicts.

The object mockups - books, packaging, signs, vehicles and the rest - are
generic and depict no one's product. The one exception is the TV: its designs
are proportioned from published dimensions of current Samsung and LG sets, and
the picture-frame design carries a faint Samsung print on its back, so the
notice above applies to it too.

## Before you publish a render

The library's MIT license covers the code. It does not grant you any right to
someone else's trademarks or product designs, and how you use a render is up
to you:

- **Follow the brand owner's guidelines.** Manufacturers publish rules for
  showing their products and marks in marketing, and they apply to a render as
  much as to a photograph.
- **Do not imply an endorsement** or a partnership you do not have.
- **Do not alter the marks.** Where a model shows a logo or wordmark, do not
  restyle it or combine it with your own branding.
- **Consider the official assets.** For advertising, app-store screenshots and
  other official placements, the manufacturer's own device frames and marketing
  resources may be required rather than any third-party mockup.

This page is general information, not legal advice. If a use matters, ask a
lawyer who can look at the specifics.
