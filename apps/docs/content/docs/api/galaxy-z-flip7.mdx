`<Flip>` renders a Galaxy Z Flip - the Z Flip 7 or Z Flip 8, chosen with
`variant`; `<FlipMockup>` is the all-in-one version. One device, two form
factors: **open** (default) fills your content onto the tall main display, and
**closed** (`openAngle={false}`) folds to the compact square whose front is
nearly all cover screen - with the two separately protruding lens rings and
flash sitting on the glass (no plate joins them, matching the retail
hardware), and the hinge band (complete with its engraved wordmark) capping
the bottom. Detail geometry (button pills, lens rings, ports, seams) follows a
reference scan of the retail Flip 7 checked against the official product
photography; the Flip 8 carries the same details on its fractionally larger,
thinner body.

```tsx
// The one-liner: its own canvas, with lighting, a ground shadow, drag-to-orbit

  <YourApp />
</FlipMockup>

// The same model with no stage of its own. Inside a canvas you arrange
// yourself it can be posed, animated, lit and joined by anything else.
<MockupCanvas>
  <Flip rotation={[0, 0.4, 0]}>
    <YourApp />
  </Flip>
</MockupCanvas>
```

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content) and, on the mockup, the
[shared mockup props](/docs/api):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `variant` | `'flip7' \| 'flip8'` | `'flip7'` | Which Galaxy Z Flip to render. The Flip 8 is a hair bigger and thinner, its main display grown to 6.9″ on the same panel grid; the cover screen is unchanged. |
| `openAngle` | `boolean \| number` | `true` | How far the clamshell is open. `true` is the unfolded tall phone (content on the main display), `false` the folded compact (content on the nearly square cover display, lens rings overlaid like cutouts). A number is degrees (0 = shut, 180 = flat) and renders the real Flex Mode pose: the halves pivot around the Armor FlexHinge while its glossy spine band (with the SAMSUNG engraving) rolls into the gap, and your content bends across the fold on two composited planes. The pose is continuous: every angle renders its own pose, and only the extremes hand over to the dedicated folded (~0°) and flat (180°) poses, which draw the crease as one seamless screen. |
| `orientation` | `'portrait' \| 'landscape'` | `'portrait'` | Landscape lays the device on its side and swaps the virtual display with upright content. |
| `color` | `string` | `'#22252b'` | Back glass / cover. Takes a retail colorway id from `FLIP_COLORWAYS[variant]`, which brings that model's frame and hinge band with it, or any CSS color - the metal is then derived from it. |
| `resolution` | `number` | per state | CSS pixel width of the active display; height follows the panel aspect. |

## The two states

Your content maps onto whichever screen is showing. Folded, the cover screen
wraps around the cameras exactly like the real FlexWindow:

<MockupExplorer
  component="FlipMockup"
  variant="flip7"

/>

## Any angle in between

`openAngle` takes a number of degrees to pose the phone at any openness - the classic half-open
Flex Mode stance, with the hinge spine rolling into the fold and your content
bending across it:

<MockupExplorer
  component="FlipMockup"
  variant="flip7"
  props={{ openAngle: 100, color: '#47536b', rotation: [0, -0.25, 0] }}
/>

## The models

The 6.85″ flagship clamshell (360×838 open, 316×349 folded). Retail finishes:
`blueshadow`, `jetblack`, `coralred`, `mint`.

## Colorways

Pass an id from that model's catalog in `FLIP_COLORWAYS` for the retail finish
- the frame and hinge band come with it. A CSS color gets metal derived from
it instead, so `color="#bfe3d0"` needs nothing else.
