`<Van>` and `<VanMockup>` render a generic delivery step van (an MT45 /
Utilimaster-class walk-in body, no brand): the shell is the side profile -
stub clamshell hood, cowl break, raked windshield, high roof, wheel-arch
cutouts - extruded across the width, with a crowned box roof, dark wheel
wells, dual rear wheels on eight-lug rims, gasketed A-pillar-raked door
glass behind a two-piece windshield with parked wipers, cab-door shut lines
and paddle handles, West-Coast mirrors, aluminium top, bottom and rub rails
with rear corner caps, a slatted roll-up rear door with its latch bar, a
chromed grille between projector headlamps, a stepped bumper with fog lamps,
license-plate recesses front and rear, corner tail lamps, marker lights, a
diamond-plate step bumper and mud flaps added on. The flat cargo side
carries a live 900×368 vinyl-wrap panel for your livery. The mockup grounds
the wheels on a virtual road plane.

## Props

Beyond the [shared screen props](/docs/screen-content):

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `color` | `string` | `'#eef0f2'` | Body paint. Wrap fleets are usually white. |
| `surfaceBackground` | `string` | `'#ffffff'` | CSS background behind your wrap content. |
| `coverage` | `'panel' \| 'full' \| 'perforated'` | `'panel'` | `'full'` covers the entire side elevations AND the whole rear face - corner post to corner post - carving out the wheel arches and the gasketed door glass, while the proud hardware (rails, mirrors, handles, markers) draws on top of the livery via per-pixel depth compositing, like hardware remounted over the vinyl; the cab-door shut lines and the box's sheet seams are carved as slits the film tucks into. On the rear, every taillight lamp, the third brake light and the shut gap around the roll-up door are carved to a slim install margin. Both coverages keep the roll-up door's slat joints visible through the wrap. `'perforated'` is the same full wrap running OVER the cab door glass as perforated window film. |
| `resolution` | `number` | `900` panel · `1360` full | Virtual side-wrap width in CSS px; the rear panel shares its dpi. |

## Regions

| Slot | Surface |
| --- | --- |
| `<VanMockup.CurbSide>` | Curb-side (+Z) wrap panel (bare children). |
| `<VanMockup.StreetSide>` | Street-side (−Z) wrap panel. |
| `<VanMockup.Rear>` | The roll-up rear door: the panel on its slats between the taillights, or the whole rear face with `coverage="full"`. |
| `<VanMockup.LicensePlate>` | Both plates - US standard 6 × 12 in, nose and tail. One slot, since a vehicle's plates carry the same registration. A string renders the built-in plate face; a node renders as-is. |

Every slot takes per-surface overrides - `surfaceBackground`,
`resolution`, `surfaceStyle` - over the mockup-level defaults. The
same slots exist on the bare `<Van>`.

With the default `coverage="panel"` all three wrap panels sit clear of the
wheel arches, lights and cab glass. Rotate the group to pose the van; the
panels stay live at any angle.

## More examples

### Full-coverage wrap with carve-outs

`coverage="full"` stretches the live panels across the entire side
elevation (rockers to roofline, tail to nose) and across the whole rear
face. A CSS `clip-path` (computed from the same dimension spec the geometry
is built from) carves the body outline, wheel arches, door glass, sheet
seams and rear taillight clusters out of the live panels, and every panel
composites per-pixel against a depth occluder shaped like that clip - so the
wheels, glass and seams show through the carves while the proud hardware
(mirrors, door handles, the body rails and corner caps, the marker lights)
draws over your livery, the way remounted hardware sits on real vinyl.
